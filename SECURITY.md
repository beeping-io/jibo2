# 🔒 Security policy

Thank you for helping keep **Jibo2** and its users safe.

## 📬 Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Instead, email
[`alfred@beeping.io`](mailto:alfred@beeping.io) with the subject
`[Jibo2 security]` and include:

- A description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce (or a proof-of-concept).
- The version / commit where you observed it.
- Any suggested remediation you have in mind.

Expect an initial acknowledgement within **72 hours**. We will keep
you in the loop until the issue is resolved.

## 🤝 Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against researchers who:

- Report vulnerabilities through the private channel above.
- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, service
  disruption and data destruction during testing.
- Give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.

## 🗓️ Disclosure

After a fix ships, we are happy to credit you in the advisory. If you
prefer to stay anonymous, let us know — we will respect that.

## 📖 Operational security

Internal rotation procedures, incident response and the secret
inventory live in [`docs/security.md`](docs/security.md).
